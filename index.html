About David Owen Plastering

A proper Bolton plasterer — not a big outfit passing you around.

I'm David. I've been plastering across Bolton since 2007, from single feature walls to full-house re-skims after damp work and extensions. Every job is done by me or one of two lads I've worked with for years — same standard, same tidy habits.

You'll get a straight answer on what your walls actually need, a fixed written price, and a finish that's ready for paint or paper. No mess left behind, no surprises on the invoice.
